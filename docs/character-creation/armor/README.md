# Burden and Armor Candidate Tables

These are intentionally oversized draft tables for cutting and combination. Individual packages and armor effects are not accepted rules yet.

## Procedure

After signature gear establishes the adventurer's +3 attribute, roll d12 on the matching table below.

The result supplies:

- a Burden explaining a pronounced weakness;
- the attribute set to −2;
- starting armor, clothing, or another form of protection;
- a candidate benefit and fictional constraint.

The remaining attribute becomes +0.

Each table has six results for either possible weak attribute, so every complete +3/+0/−2 arrangement is equally represented.

## Candidate armor vocabulary

These terms are provisional:

- **Reinforced:** +1 maximum HP while worn. Putting armor on never restores already-lost HP.
- **Guard:** once per fight, prevent 1 damage from a qualifying hit, then mark the armor spent until the fight ends.
- **Resist [tag]:** the first qualifying hit each fight deals 0 damage.
- **Insulated [tag]:** a qualifying Wound still causes universal disadvantage but not its additional source-specific acute effect.
- **Stable:** ordinary effects cannot knock the wearer prone or move them against their will.

Because adventurers have only 2 HP and ordinary attacks deal 1 damage, flat permanent damage reduction is not suitable: reducing every hit by 1 would negate ordinary danger.

## Cutting and balance guidance

- Keep six results for either possible −2 attribute within each +3 table, or deliberately document a different distribution.
- Treat the −2 attribute as the main mechanical cost; avoid adding severe universal penalties to every package.
- Do not let protection erase the source-specific Wound system.
- Remember that +1 HP or one prevented damage extends the ordinary sequence before a Wound from three hits to four.
- Decide whether Reinforced and Guard can stack with sword parrying or shield benefits.
- Preserve some mundane results so strange armor remains surprising.
- Ensure a lone adventurer can use every benefit without requiring an adjacent ally.
