# Playtest 006: Two Lights Before Darkness

> **Lighting update:** This playtest used the now-superseded independent d6 Lightable-room procedure. Current rooms use template-authored Lighting metadata. Its two-adventurer, Wound, Skill Token, darkness, and travel findings remain useful, but Room 5 would not now become Lightable merely from a d6 result.

## Purpose

Run two newly generated adventurers through five Haunted Tomb rooms and extraction using the current rules. Identify whether the core game is playable and which procedures remain unresolved.

The test included:

- one shared starting torch;
- one additional torch chosen as an adventurer item;
- Lightable rooms;
- darkness disadvantage;
- scaled hostile opposition;
- monster Instincts;
- Wounds, safe treatment, and Scars;
- known-room travel and wandering-enemy rolls;
- Skill Token spending.

No unfavorable result was rerolled.

## Lightable rooms

The d6 results for rooms 1–5 were:

`5, 5, 3, 6, 1`

Only Room 5 was Lightable. The party reached it after both portable torches expired, so they had no flame with which to light its fixtures.

## Room 1 — Drowned Vigil

Entering consumed turn 1.

Adventurer 1 spent the starting Skill Token and invoked the Witch Hunter Past. The roll was 11 + 5 = 16 against Standard 12. The correct rite durably resolved the 4 HP wailing choir without combat.

The room was conquered, the Book of Last Rites was recovered, and both adventurers gained a Skill Token.

## Room 2 — Bridge of Borrowed Faces

Entering consumed turn 2. Adventurer 2 crossed the bridge under pressure with 16 + 3 = 19.

Four 1 HP bone scuttlers opposed the pair. Attack rolls were:

`8, 12, 16, 20, 5, 1, 3, 18`

The first attack failed before the colony had a locked target, so it damaged the nearest adventurer. The second attack succeeded; Adventurer 2 became the colony's first attacker and therefore its locked target.

Two later failures reduced Adventurer 2 to 0 HP. A natural 1 consumed turn 3. The next failure allowed the scuttlers to attack the exposed target and inflict a **Gnawed Wound**. The final roll defeated the last scuttler.

After immediate danger ended, HP recovered but the Wound remained. Adventurer 1 used the medkit in safety. Treatment consumed turn 4, converted the Wound into a Scar, and reduced Adventurer 2's Strength from −2 to −3.

Room 2 was conquered and both adventurers gained Skill Tokens.

## Travel to Room 3

Crossing conquered Room 2 consumed turn 5; its wandering-enemy roll was 2. Crossing Room 1 consumed turn 6; its roll was 3. The starting torch expired.

The additional torch was lit.

## Room 3 — The Accusing Ring

Entering consumed turn 7.

Adventurer 1 spent a Skill Token to invoke the Witch Hunter Past and expose the substituted royal remains. The roll was 14 + 5 = 19 against Hard 15. The accusing poltergeist was durably resolved without fighting its potential 4 HP profile.

The room was conquered and both adventurers gained Skill Tokens.

## Room 4 — Furnace of Unnamed Hands

Travel consumed three turns:

- Room 3 on turn 8, danger roll 4;
- Room 1 on turn 9, danger roll 6;
- Room 2 on turn 10, danger roll 6.

Entering Room 4 consumed turn 11.

Adventurer 1 spent a Skill Token and used the Witch Hunter Past to identify and follow the risen embalmers' sealed protocol. The roll was 19 + 5 = 24 against Hard 15. The potential 4 HP opposition did not attack.

Room 4 was conquered and both adventurers gained Skill Tokens.

## Room 5 — Shattered Memorial Maze

Crossing Room 2 consumed turn 12; its wandering-enemy roll was 6. The second torch expired.

Entering Room 5 consumed turn 13. The room was Lightable, but the party had no remaining flame, so it remained dark.

Adventurer 1 invoked the Witch Hunter Past and used the Book of Last Rites to interpret the memorial. The Book's advantage and darkness disadvantage cancelled. The first roll was 6 + 5 = 11 against Hard 15. The returning walls dealt 1 damage.

A second Skill Token funded another attempt. The roll was 7 + 5 = 12. The walls dealt another damage, reducing Adventurer 1 from 3 to 1 HP.

With no Skill Tokens or portable light remaining, the pair abandoned the unconquered room.

## Extraction

Crossing Room 2 in darkness consumed turn 14; its wandering-enemy roll was 3. Crossing Room 1 consumed turn 15; its roll was 4. No wandering enemies appeared.

The adventurers exited. Adventurer 1's HP recovered after escaping the memorial danger.

## Findings

### Two-adventurer scaling produced credible danger

Four 1 HP scuttlers required enough successful attacks to inflict a natural Wound. The pair survived because they had a medkit and reached safety before another Wound.

### A strong Past could bypass several hostile profiles

The Witch Hunter Past resolved Rooms 1, 3, and 4. This was fictionally appropriate, cost Skill Tokens, and demonstrated that higher monster HP does not force combat. It also shows how much a well-matched Past can dominate a themed site.

### Two torches supported four rooms, not five

The extra torch was a strong preparation choice. Portable light still ended before Room 5 and extraction. Darkness directly caused the retreat.

### Lightable does not guarantee usable light

Room 5 contained fixtures but could not be lit without a remaining flame. This made lightability and portable fuel distinct resources.

### Wandering enemies remain under-tested

All eight eligible d6 rolls were safe. Lit-room suppression and wandering combat therefore need additional fixtures despite having defined procedures.

### Skill Token pressure differed between characters

Adventurer 1 repeatedly spent tokens on the highly relevant Past. Adventurer 2 reached the three-token cap and never found a compelling use for Appraiser's Eye. Character options need enough opportunities to prevent some builds from routinely wasting conquest rewards.

## Rules still unresolved

The core expedition loop is playable, but the full game is not complete. Important open rules include:

1. concise terrain effects for pits, water, rubble, balconies, bridges, falling, and forced movement;
2. lantern fuel, extinguishing, and relighting;
3. group traversal checks and who suffers consequences when one adventurer leads;
4. exact monster special attacks beyond ordinary automatic damage;
5. more Domain, Theme, monster, Wound, and Wandering Enemy content;
6. final armor, weapon, Knack, and starting-item catalogs;
7. rations, resting, recovery, Scar healing, and the exact boundary restoring HP;
8. solo oracle procedures;
9. advancement, campaign recovery, treasure value, and settlement play;
10. complete rules presentation and the supporting web app.

## Recommended next decision

Define concise terrain effects. The bridge check in Room 2 still required an ad hoc ruling, and several authored templates depend on pits, water, rubble, elevation, and forced movement.
