# Playtest 004: The Choir Chose Its Mourner

## Purpose

Run the four adventurers generated for Playtest 003 through a longer five-room expedition and extraction after adding narrative monster Instincts.

The test covered:

- scaled hostile opposition;
- persistent target locks;
- manipulating Instinct through position and first contact;
- Wounds, emergency treatment, and death;
- noncombat conquest of hostile rooms;
- reawakened conquered-room danger;
- Skill Token flow;
- light use across an extended route.

No unfavorable roll was rerolled.

## Route and opposition

The route was:

`Room 1 → Room 2 → Room 1 → Room 3 → Room 1 → Room 2 → Room 4 → Room 2 → Room 5 → Room 2 → Room 1 → exit`

Four adventurers entered Room 1, so its hostile-opposition floor was 8 HP. Adventurer 1 died there. Later hostile rooms used the three-survivor floor of 6 HP.

| Room | Main challenge | Opposition or target |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Wailing choir | 8 HP collective threat; fixates on whoever interrupts or leads its rite |
| 2 | Bone scuttlers | Six separate 1 HP creatures; colony locks onto the first adventurer to harm it |
| 3 | Accusing poltergeist | 6 HP if fought; targets whoever manipulates its anchor |
| 4 | Risen embalmers | Three 2 HP creatures if fought; enforce their sealed preparation protocol |
| 5 | Returning memorial walls | Hard 15 environmental obstacle; no hostile creatures |

- one 2 HP spectral double in Room 2;
- three 1 HP drowned hands in Room 1;
- three 1 HP bone scuttlers during extraction.

## Room 1 — Drowned Vigil

Entering consumed exploration turn 1.

Adventurer 1 spent their starting Skill Token and invoked the Witch Hunter Past to restore the rite. The roll was 4 + 5 = 9 against Standard 12. On failure, the choir fixed its Instinct on the person who interrupted the vigil and attacked them for 1 damage, reducing HP from 2 to 1.

Adventurer 4 repeatedly loaded and fired the hand crossbow:

| Attack | Natural roll | Result | Choir HP | Dungeon consequence |
| ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 14 | Hit for 2 | 6 | — |
| 2 | 20 | Critical hit for 3 | 3 | — |
| 3 | 2 | Failure | 3 | Choir attacks locked target: 1 → 0 HP |
| 4 | 14 | Hit for 2 | 1 | — |
| 5 | 1 | Critical failure | 1 | Turn 2 passes; choir gives Adventurer 1 a Disoriented Wound |

The party chose emergency treatment rather than risk another attack. Adventurer 2 spent their starting Skill Token to invoke Apprentice to an Apothecary. The treatment roll was 2 + 2 = 4 against the temporary Standard 12 difficulty.

Treatment failed, consuming exploration turn 3 but not the medkit. The choir attacked its locked target again. Adventurer 1 received a second active Wound and died.

Adventurer 2 then rolled 15 + 3 = 18 and dealt the choir's final point of damage. Room 1 was conquered. The three survivors each gained a Skill Token and recovered the dead adventurer's portable items, including the unused medkit and alchemist's satchel. Recovering or carrying the body was outside this test.

## Room 2 — Bridge of Borrowed Faces

Entering consumed turn 4. Adventurer 3 led the pressured bridge crossing and rolled 19 + 3 = 22.

Before the colony had been harmed, three consecutive failed attacks rolled 3, 2, and 7. The nearest front-line adventurer was Adventurer 2, who took all three attacks and fell from 3 to 0 HP.

Adventurer 3 then deliberately attacked and rolled 13 + 3 = 16, defeating the first scuttler and becoming the colony's locked target. The party's next five attacks rolled 13, 15, 14, 16, and 10. All succeeded against Standard 12 and defeated the remaining five scuttlers before the colony received another action.

Room 2 was conquered. Immediate danger ended, Adventurer 2 recovered to 3 HP, and every survivor gained a Skill Token.

The encounter showed that the party could deliberately choose who acquired a known target lock, but only after someone successfully harmed the colony.

## First reawakened danger

Crossing conquered Room 2 consumed turn 5. Its danger roll was 1, stopping travel and creating a 2 HP spectral double of Adventurer 2. Adventurer 4 rolled 10 + 3 = 13 and defeated it with one loaded crossbow hit. The danger granted no Skill Tokens.

Crossing conquered Room 1 consumed turn 6. Its danger roll was 2, so no danger appeared. The first torch expired.

## Room 3 — The Accusing Ring

Entering consumed turn 7 and included the first ring traversal.

Adventurer 2 used the counterweighted bier to pin the haunting's anchor, rolling 13 + 3 = 16 against Hard 15. The room was conquered without attacking the poltergeist's 6 HP. Each survivor gained a Skill Token.

The hostile-opposition floor remained a combat fallback rather than a requirement to resolve the room through damage.

## Travel toward Room 4

Recrossing Room 3 consumed turn 8; its danger roll was 5.

Crossing Room 1 consumed turn 9; its danger roll was 1. Three drowned hands animated in the flooded channel and pursued Adventurer 2, who carried the Book of Last Rites.

The party's attacks rolled 12, 6, 10, 1, and 20 against Standard 12. Three succeeded and defeated the hands. The two failures dealt 2 total damage to Adventurer 2. The natural 1 consumed additional turn 10. HP recovered when the danger ended.

Crossing Room 2 consumed turn 11; its danger roll was 3.

## Room 4 — Furnace of Unnamed Hands

Entering consumed turn 12. The second torch expired.

Adventurer 2 spent 1 Skill Token to invoke Apprentice to an Apothecary and follow the risen embalmers' sealed preparation protocol. The roll was 15 + 2 = 17 against Hard 15.

The embalmers accepted the correct procedure and did not attack. This durably resolved the room's creature-plus-Domain challenge without fighting its potential 6 HP opposition. Each survivor gained a Skill Token.

## Room 5 — Shattered Memorial Maze

Crossing Room 2 consumed turn 13; its danger roll was 6. The lantern was lit.

Entering Room 5 consumed turn 14. Adventurer 4 used the Book of Last Rites and their Standard-Bearer Past to interpret the memorial order. The Book granted advantage; the Past's +2 training offset Mind −2 for a total of +0.

The first attempt rolled 13 and 10, retaining 13 and failing against Hard 15. The returning walls dealt 1 damage. Adventurer 4 spent another Skill Token and retried, rolling 16 and 10. The retained 16 succeeded, permanently aligned the route, and revealed the Quest lead.

Room 5 was conquered. HP recovered and the survivors gained Skill Tokens.

## Extraction

Crossing Room 2 consumed turn 15. Its danger roll was 1, producing three new bone scuttlers.

Adventurer 2's opening attack rolled 3 and failed. Before the colony had a locked target, it attacked the nearest adventurer and reduced Adventurer 2 from 3 to 2 HP.

Adventurer 3 then rolled 18, defeated one scuttler, and acquired the colony's target lock. The sabre's Mobile benefit repositioned Adventurer 3 behind Adventurer 2. Adventurer 4 rolled 16 and defeated the second scuttler.

The next attack rolled 8 and failed. The final scuttler could not reach its locked target past the interposing Adventurer 2, so its dungeon action changed position instead of dealing damage. The following attack rolled 2 and failed; now in reach, the scuttler attacked Adventurer 3 for 1 damage. A final roll of 10 + 3 defeated it. HP recovered after the fight.

Crossing Room 1 consumed turn 16. Its danger roll was 6. The three survivors exited with two turns of lantern light remaining.

## What worked

### Instinct produced distinct, legible tactics

The choir's uninterrupted target lock caused a death. The scuttlers' first-harm lock let the party deliberately choose a target. The sabre's repositioning then made the scuttler spend an action reaching that target rather than dealing automatic damage.

### Position constrained target locks

Instinct did not permit attacks through an interposing adventurer. The monster retained its preferred target but had to use an action to regain access.

### The game was lethal without every room becoming combat

One of four adventurers died in the opening room. Rooms 3 and 4 were then conquered through their anchor and protocol despite having 6 HP combat profiles.

### Reawakened rooms made extraction materially dangerous

Three of eight conquered-room danger rolls produced new threats. They caused additional attacks, one natural 1, and meaningful light use without granting more conquest rewards.

### Light became relevant over the longer route

Two six-turn torches were insufficient after natural 1s, failed treatment, and repeated traversal. The chosen lantern made continued exploration and extraction possible.

## What strained

### Reawakened-danger strength is undefined

The test improvised one 2 HP double and groups with 1 HP per active adventurer. Applying the full 2 HP-per-adventurer room floor to every danger roll may make backtracking too combat-heavy, but using a lone 1 HP threat would be trivial.

### Crossbow reload is not a meaningful cost in the fluid turn system

Reloading requires a separate adventurer action, but a no-roll action does not surrender control. The crossbow therefore repeatedly dealt 2 damage with no systemic reload cost beyond fiction. It dominated the 8 HP choir fight.

### Target-lock lethality is steep

Once the choir drove its 2 HP target to 0, a failed attack caused a Wound and failed emergency treatment caused death. This was understandable and telegraphed, but left little recovery margin.

### HP loss still disappears abruptly

Adventurer 2 reached 0 HP in Room 2 and later took multiple hits from reawakened dangers, but carried no harm between them because each danger ended before another hit caused a Wound.

### Party-size scaling shrinks after a death

The opposition floor fell from 8 to 6 HP after the first death. This supports parties of different sizes, but also softens the expedition immediately after a loss. More tests are needed to determine whether that is welcome or feels like visible difficulty correction.

## Recommended next decisions

1. Define the strength and form of danger generated when a conquered-room d6 rolls 1.
2. Replace the crossbow's nominal no-roll reload action with a cost meaningful under fluid turns.
3. Define provisional starting light using the sixteen-turn expedition as evidence.
4. Add Instincts to future monsters, including explicit conditions for losing or changing a locked target.
5. Test a high-damage alternating-target monster against this party model.

## Post-test decision

The first recommendation was resolved after the playtest. A 1 on a known-room danger roll now introduces wandering enemies from a site-specific table. An ordinary wandering group has 1 total HP per active adventurer, minimum 2, and remains in the dungeon if escaped. The spectral double and drowned hands used above remain historical ad hoc interpretations rather than the current wandering procedure.
