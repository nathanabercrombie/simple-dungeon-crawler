# Playtest 005: The Light Failed at the Memorial

## Purpose

Correct Playtest 004 after discovering that its hand crossbow and buckler had incorrectly received the full crossbow's 2-damage Loaded Shot. Replay the same party and rooms with:

- proper 1-damage hand-crossbow attacks;
- pressured reload checks;
- one starting six-turn torch;
- one-third Lightable room generation;
- darkness disadvantage;
- current Instinct, opposition, treatment, and travel rules.

No unfavorable roll was rerolled.

- **Party-generation seed:** 20260402
- **Action seed:** 20260403

For this test, the lantern could be extinguished and relit in a lit room. That behavior remains a temporary ruling.

## Room 2 — Bridge of Borrowed Faces

Entering consumed turn 4. Room 2 rolled Lightable and its fixtures were lit immediately. The bridge check rolled 10 + 3 = 13.

Six 1 HP scuttlers opposed the three survivors. Eleven attacks produced these natural rolls:

`2, 10, 5, 13, 20, 3, 12, 6, 10, 1, 20`

The first attack failed, damaging front-line Adventurer 2. The second succeeded and made Adventurer 3 the colony's locked target. Later failures let the scuttlers reach and expose Adventurer 3. The natural 1 consumed turn 5 and inflicted a **Gnawed Wound**. The final natural 20 defeated the last scuttler.

After danger ended, HP recovered but the Wound remained. The party used the recovered medkit in the lit room. Safe treatment consumed turn 6, converted the Wound into a Scar, and reduced Adventurer 3's Strength from +0 to −1. The starting torch expired. Room 2's persistent light provided safety and prevented future wandering rolls there.

## Room 3 — The Accusing Ring

Crossing lit Room 2 consumed turn 7 without a danger roll. The lantern was used for the unlit route through Room 1 on turn 8; its danger roll was 3. Entering Room 3 consumed turn 9.

Adventurer 2 rolled 15 + 3 = 18 against Hard 15 and pinned the haunting's anchor with the bier. Room 3 was conquered without fighting its 6 HP profile. The survivors gained Skill Tokens.

## Room 4 — Furnace of Unnamed Hands

Crossing Room 3 consumed turn 10; its danger roll was 6. Crossing Room 1 consumed turn 11; its danger roll was 4. Crossing lit Room 2 consumed turn 12 without a danger roll.

The lantern was conserved where persistent room light allowed and supplied its last required turn while entering Lightable Room 4 on turn 13. The party lit its fixtures immediately.

Adventurer 2 invoked Apprentice to an Apothecary. The first protocol roll was 10 + 2 = 12 against Hard 15; the embalmers attacked their violator for 1 damage. A retry rolled 16 + 2 = 18 and durably satisfied the protocol. Room 4 was conquered without combat and the survivors gained Skill Tokens.

## Room 5 — Shattered Memorial Maze

Crossing lit Room 2 consumed turn 14 without a danger roll. Entering unlightable Room 5 consumed turn 15. Portable light was exhausted, so every check had darkness disadvantage.

The Book of Last Rites would normally grant advantage to the Standard-Bearer's memorial interpretation, but darkness cancelled it. The Past's +2 training offset Mind −2, producing a flat d20 against Hard 15.

Three attempts rolled 10, 2, and 3. All failed. The returning walls dealt 1 damage after each failure, reducing Adventurer 4 from 3 HP to 0. Three Skill Tokens had been spent across the attempts.

The party abandoned the unconquered room rather than risk a Wound in darkness. Retreat to lit Room 2 consumed turn 16 and restored HP when immediate danger ended.

## Extraction

Crossing unlit Room 1 in darkness consumed turn 17. Its wandering-enemy roll was 5, so the party exited without another encounter. Room 2's light suppressed all of its potential wandering rolls during the expedition.

## Findings

### Pressured reload worked

The hand crossbow's reload checks created real openings. One failed reload let the choir attack its locked target. Switching to already-ready melee weapons became attractive instead of reloading automatically after every shot.

### The hand-crossbow package was no longer dominant

Its shots dealt the correct 1 damage. The package retained Near range and +1 maximum HP but did not erase the distinction between it and the full 2-damage crossbow.

### Persistent light changed route safety

Lit Room 2 prevented several wandering rolls, provided a safe treatment location, and served as the party's retreat destination. Lightable rooms materially changed the map rather than merely improving a single check.

### One torch made found or chosen light essential

The starting torch expired after Room 2 treatment. The additional lantern enabled Rooms 3 and 4. When portable light finally ended, darkness defeated the party's attempt to conquer Room 5 despite a relevant book and three Skill Tokens.

### Darkness created a credible retreat decision

The party could continue rolling at disadvantage, but three failures drove one adventurer to 0 HP. Retreating to a lit room was preferable to waiting for a Wound.

### The full crossbow still needs direct testing

This test validated the reload procedure on a 1-damage hand crossbow. It did not test whether a full crossbow's 2 damage is balanced by requiring both a pressured reload check and an attack check.

## Recommended next decisions

1. Define lantern duration, fuel, and whether lanterns can be extinguished and relit.
2. Continue with concise terrain effects.
3. Directly test the full 2-damage crossbow against a great weapon.
