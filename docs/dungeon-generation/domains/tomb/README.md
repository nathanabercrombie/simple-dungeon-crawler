# Domain Card: Tomb

Tombs preserve identity, status, remains, memory, and obligations between the living and dead.

Roll d6 separately in a column when the room procedure calls for that kind of detail.

| d6 | Function | Fixture | Actor | Valuable | Security |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Processional threshold | Monumental doors and worn tracks | Mourner or funerary attendant | Funeral coins and ceremonial cloth | Passage requires the correct approach or gesture |
| 2 | Preparation chamber | Stone slab, drains, hooks, and basins | Embalmer, surgeon, or assistant | Preservative resins, surgical tools, rare salts | Toxic vapor, contaminated tools, or a sealed work protocol |
| 3 | Common crypt | Burial niches, biers, and name plaques | Descendant, grave robber, or scavenger | Personal grave goods and family tokens | False niche, weighted slab, or alarm for disturbed remains |
| 4 | Ossuary | Bone stacks, sorting shelves, and skull walls | Bonekeeper, vermin colony, or collector | Saintly remains, ivory, and bone charms | Unstable stacks, false assembly, or guardian made from mixed bones |
| 5 | Memorial shrine | Effigies, offering bowls, lamps, and inscriptions | Priest, pilgrim, petitioner, or caretaker | Votive jewels, sacred oil, and recorded secrets | Offerings must balance, names must be spoken, or rites followed in order |
| 6 | Royal or hidden sepulcher | Sarcophagus, raised dais, funerary statues, and sealed annex | Interred ruler, sworn guardian, heir, or rival claimant | Regalia, relic weapon, spellbook, or proof of lineage | Curse, oath, guardian, decoy burial, or identity test |

## Domain questions

When interpretation is uncertain, ask:

- Whose memory or status was this room built to preserve?
- What burial practice is visible?
- What would count as desecration here?
- Who still benefits from the tomb remaining sealed?
- Which object matters for reasons beyond its monetary value?
