# Worked Vertical Slice: Haunted Tomb

This page demonstrates how the reusable [Tomb Domain](../../domains/tomb/README.md) and [Haunted Theme](../../themes/haunted/README.md) combine with universal room generation. It is not a required Haunted Tomb table.

## Site identity

- **Domain:** Tomb
- **Theme:** Haunted
- **Escalation:** 2 unless the specific site says otherwise
- **Danger Rank:** supplied by each room template
- **Recurring question:** What obligation between living and dead remains unresolved?

## Translating Challenge results

These are examples of interpretation, not fixed outcomes.

| d12 Challenge | Haunted Tomb interpretation |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 — Quiet aftermath | An interrupted rite, opened grave, extinguished vigil, or evidence that a ghost just reenacted its death |
| 2 — Domain obstacle | Sealed sarcophagus, blocked processional route, misidentified remains, or monumental burial architecture obstructs passage |
| 3 — Theme hazard | Life-draining cold, possessive whispers, violent reenactment, flying objects, or a room animated by collective grief |
| 4 — Trap | A Tomb Security result triggers or has been altered by a Haunted Manifestation |
| 5 — Puzzle | Restore names, offerings, bones, lineage, funeral order, or the true sequence of a remembered death |
| 6 — Social obstacle | A ghost, mourner, robber, heir, caretaker, or possessed survivor demands recognition, truth, restitution, or secrecy |
| 7 — Uncertain creature | An unquiet dead person, tomb scavenger, psychopomp, or guardian may bargain before becoming violent |
| 8 — Hostile creature | Risen guard, mourning shade, bone swarm, possessed statue, or grave robber driven mad by whispers |
| 9 — Creature + Domain | A guardian uses burial architecture, ritual law, false graves, or funerary machinery |
| 10 — Creature + Theme | A mundane Tomb Actor is possessed, duplicated, reenacted, or supported by a room-scale haunting |
| 11 — Compound | Two of the above share one anchor or competing conquest conditions |
| 12 — Major threat | Named revenant, royal ghost, mass haunting, funerary colossus, or severe curse tied to the site's central wrong |

## Translating Opportunity results

| d8 Opportunity | Haunted Tomb interpretation |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 — None | Passage and any conquest Skill Token are the only gains |
| 2 — Clue | Epitaph, ghostly reenactment, family record, disturbed remains, or confession about the central wrong |
| 3 — Supply | Lamp oil, bandages, embalming salts, incense, antivenom, tools, or preserved rations |
| 4 — Usable feature | Counterweight, bier, funeral lift, movable statue, offering mechanism, or ghost-commanded fixture |
| 5 — Treasure | Funeral coins, jewelry, regalia, sacred oil, relic bones, or grave goods |
| 6 — Secret | False niche, decoy coffin, erased name, concealed processional door, or hidden mourner's passage |
| 7 — Shortcut | Funeral lift, ossuary crawlway, ghost path, royal stair, or passage opened by completing a rite |
| 8 — Major discovery | Relic weapon, spellbook, proof of lineage, named remains, royal seal, or anchor tied to the site's primary haunting |

## Sample threats

All use the room's Danger Rank as Defense unless noted.

| Threat | HP | Behavior | Instinct | Wound |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Bone scuttler | 1 | Swarms through niches and carries small objects into bone piles | Swarm the first adventurer who harms the colony; remain on that target until it dies, escapes, or becomes unreachable | **Gnawed:** ongoing bleeding or embedded fragments; treated Scar reduces Strength |
| Mourning shade | 1 | Passes through barriers and isolates whoever resembles a remembered person | Choose the adventurer who most resembles someone from its memory; pass through obstacles to isolate them | **Soul-Chilled:** cannot benefit from ordinary warmth; treated Scar reduces Mind |
| Risen guard | 2 | Holds a threshold and follows the wording of an ancient oath | Attack whoever violates the guarded threshold or oath; stop when its exact duty no longer permits pursuit | **Run Through:** severe bleeding; treated Scar reduces Strength |
| Wailing choir | 2 | Acts as one group and attacks through sound and remembered grief | Fixate on whoever interrupts, contradicts, or attempts to lead its rite | **Disoriented:** speech and concentration fracture; treated Scar reduces Mind |
| Possessed funerary statue | 3 | Uses pillars, dais, and sarcophagi as part of its body | Crush whoever touches the protected tomb or damages its architectural body | **Crushed:** impaired movement and breath; treated Scar reduces Strength |
| Royal revenant | 5 | Commands lesser dead and demands obedience to obsolete law | Condemn the bearer of stolen regalia, the false claimant, or the highest-ranking intruder | **Death-Marked:** visions and compulsions tied to the royal line; treated Scar reduces Mind |

## Haunted Tomb wandering enemies

| d6 | Wanderers | Instinct |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Grave rats | Swarm whoever carries food, remains, or an active bleeding Wound |
| 2 | Bone scuttlers | Lock onto the first adventurer who harms the colony |
| 3 | Shambling mourners | Pursue the nearest living creature without changing targets while it remains reachable |
| 4 | Mourning shades | Isolate whoever most resembles someone they remember |
| 5 | Risen burial patrol | Attack whoever lacks the proper funerary sign, name, or gesture |
| 6 | Grave-ogre | Attack the loudest or most heavily burdened adventurer |

An ordinary result has at least 1 total HP per active adventurer, minimum 2. The grave-ogre and other specific entries may exceed that floor.

## Four generated examples

### Pillared memorial gallery

- Danger Rank: Standard 12
- Challenge: creature plus Domain complication
- Opportunity: clue
- Domain Function: memorial shrine
- Theme Manifestation: ghost repeats a task

Spectral attendants carry invisible offerings between pillars and attack anyone crossing their route. Their repeated gestures point toward an effigy whose erased inscription names the tomb's betrayer.

### Sunken preparation chamber

- Danger Rank: Hard 15
- Challenge: trap
- Opportunity: shortcut
- Domain Function: preparation chamber
- Theme Anchor: murder weapon

Disturbing the embalming slab seals the doors and fills the floor pit with preservative fluid. Returning the hidden ritual knife to its drain opens a service stair used to move bodies between levels.

### Split-level ossuary

- Danger Rank: Easy 9
- Challenge: quiet aftermath
- Opportunity: secret
- Domain Function: ossuary
- Theme Sign: shadows act independently

Bone stacks have recently collapsed away from one wall, although nothing is present. A shadow points to a skull whose jaw contains the catch for a false panel. No challenge means no conquest reward unless investigation awakens or reveals one.

### Royal sepulcher with balcony

- Danger Rank: Extreme 18
- Challenge: compound puzzle and social obstacle
- Opportunity: major discovery
- Domain Function: royal sepulcher
- Theme Drive: force someone to confess the truth

The royal ghost demands that the correct heir stand beneath the balcony while six funerary statues are turned toward the liar. Solving the lineage arrangement exposes the confession and opens the sarcophagus containing a relic and the anchor of the wider haunting.

## What this slice tests

- Whether compact six-row cards provide enough specificity.
- Whether Challenge and Opportunity remain actionable after modular combination.
- Whether Domain, Theme, terrain, and Danger Rank all materially contribute.
- Whether a solo player receives enough evidence to interpret the result fairly.
- Whether repeated rooms feel related without becoming repetitive.

The [King Who Was Buried Twice](objective/README.md) adds a worked far-edge Heart, three optional Revelations, and an extraction journey to test a hybrid spatial/progress objective.
