# Example Objective: The King Who Was Buried Twice

This is an illustrative hybrid objective for a standard 4×4 Haunted Tomb. The pre-placed Revelation markers are superseded as a general procedure by the escalating [Quest generator](../../../quests/README.md), but the Revelation effects remain a useful worked example of quest elements changing a Heart.

## Setup

- Place the entrance in sector B4 on the southern edge.
- Place the **Heart**, the Royal Sepulcher, in sector B1 on the opposite edge.
- Randomly place three facedown **Revelation** markers in non-entrance, non-Heart sectors.
- A Revelation is integrated into that room's normal Opportunity when the room is generated; it does not replace the Challenge.
- Two Revelations allow the Heart to be resolved through the intended rite. The third improves the reward.

Example discovered map:

```text
       A        B        C        D
   +--------+--------+--------+--------+
1  |        | HEART  |        |        |
   +--------+--------+--------+--------+
2  |  ??    | T-07   |  R?    |        |
   +--------+--------+--------+--------+
3  | T-05 R | T-01   | T-12 R |  ??    |
   +--------+--------+--------+--------+
4  |        |   EN   |        |        |
   +--------+--------+--------+--------+
```

- `EN`: entrance.
- `HEART`: objective sector.
- `T-##`: generated room template.
- `R`: found Revelation.
- `R?`: connected sector containing an unrevealed marker.
- `??`: reachable unexplored sector.

## The three Revelations

### The Stolen Name

The person in the royal sarcophagus was buried under the king's name, but is not the king. Evidence may appear in an epitaph, family record, ghostly confession, or mismatched remains.

**Heart effect:** speaking the true identity prevents the mass haunting from defending the false corpse.

### The Murder Weapon

The ceremonial knife used during embalming is also the weapon that killed the true king. It remains a physical anchor for the reenacted betrayal.

**Heart effect:** returning or destroying it ends the room's repeating death scene and disables its hazard.

### The Broken Oath

The royal guard knowingly protected the substitution to prevent a civil war. The exact wording of its oath contains a release clause.

**Heart effect:** reciting the clause turns the risen guards neutral and permits parley with the revenant.

## Reaching the Heart

The Heart is geographically reachable whenever the map connects to B1. Revelations change the available approaches rather than functioning as an invisible keycard.

### With no Revelations

- Royal revenant, wailing choir, and risen guards are all active.
- The reenacted murder is an Extreme 18 room hazard.
- A direct fight or an exceptionally clever unanticipated solution remains possible.

### With one Revelation

- Resolve the matching layer: mass haunting, murder reenactment, or risen guards.
- The remaining Heart challenge is still severe.

### With two Revelations

- The adventurers can perform the intended exposure-and-release rite.
- The final confrontation can be a Hard 15 puzzle, negotiation, or focused fight rather than the full compound threat.
- Conquering the Heart completes the site's primary objective.

### With all three Revelations

- The rite becomes Standard 12.
- The party can safely claim the uncursed **Crown of the Last Vigil**, an exceptional relic.
- The true history is known well enough to change a faction, bloodline, or settlement after the expedition.

## Extraction

Conquering the Heart does not teleport the party to safety. They must return to the entrance through the generated map before light expires. Previously conquered rooms remain changed by play, and unresolved branches remain optional.

## Why this is hybrid

- **Spatial:** the Heart has a known far-edge sector and must be physically reached.
- **Procedural:** Revelation locations, intervening rooms, Challenges, and Opportunities emerge during play.
- **Progressive:** exploration removes layers from the final threat.
- **Not a hard gate:** players may confront the Heart early and seek other free-form solutions.
- **Replayable:** the same structure can use seals, machine parts, witnesses, faction support, ritual acts, or monster weaknesses in another Domain and Theme.
