# Theme Card: Haunted

Haunted sites retain people, emotions, duties, and violent moments that should have ended.

Roll d6 separately in a column when the room procedure calls for that kind of detail.

| d6 | Sign | Manifestation | Drive | Anchor | Boon |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Frost, visible breath, and dead lantern flames | A ghost repeats a route or task | Complete an unfinished duty | Unattended remains | Warning about a nearby danger |
| 2 | Whispers using names no one introduced | A living creature becomes briefly possessed | Force someone to confess the truth | Written name, signature, or epitaph | A truthful answer about the past |
| 3 | Objects return to old positions when unobserved | A poltergeist hurls, locks, or rearranges objects | Drive intruders from claimed ground | Cherished possession | Temporary command of a moving object or mechanism |
| 4 | A violent moment flickers at the edge of sight | The room reenacts a past event | Prevent, avenge, or repeat a betrayal | Murder weapon or bloodstain | A sensory vision revealing what happened |
| 5 | Reflections and shadows act independently | A spectral double imitates a living person | Replace, accuse, or test the living | Portrait, mirror, or funerary likeness | Passage through a barrier the ghost ignores |
| 6 | Many faint voices speak as one | A collective haunting animates the room itself | Receive proper recognition or burial | Desecrated threshold, altar, or mass grave | Safe rest or protection after the correct rite |

## Theme questions

When interpretation is uncertain, ask:

- Who or what cannot leave?
- What emotion is strongest here?
- What action does the haunting repeat?
- What would satisfy, weaken, or enrage it?
- Which physical anchor makes an incorporeal problem actionable?
