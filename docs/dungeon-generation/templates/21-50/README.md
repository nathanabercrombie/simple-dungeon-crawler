# Room Templates 21–50: Deep and Extreme Pools

These exact-grid prototypes complete the fifty-template master list required by every default site size and escalation pairing.

- Templates 21–32: Hard 15
- Templates 33–50: Extreme 18

All templates fit within a 9×9 footprint, excluding the surrounding sector margin. `E` is the initial attachment point; every `X`, `U`, and `D` is another potential route. Contents remain separate.

## Additional legend

Use the [opening-pool legend](../01-20/README.md) plus:

| Mark | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `C` | Cage, cell bars, or containment structure |
| `F` | Furnace, flame jet, or intense heat source |
| `G` | Large mechanism, gear, valve, or moving apparatus |
| `L` | Lava or similarly lethal liquid |
| `M` | Mirror, reflective wall, or deceptive surface |
| `T` | Throne, engine core, altar, or other central objective fixture |
| `U` | Visible upward connection |

## Playtest questions

- Are eighteen Extreme templates too many, or does the sliding range introduce them late enough?
- Which layouts are visually distinct but tactically redundant?
- Do five-exit rooms create useful late-site loops or excessive frontier growth?
- Are mechanisms and special terrain legible without assigning universal effects too early?
- Should template 50 remain a generic room, or be reserved when a Heart result occurs?
