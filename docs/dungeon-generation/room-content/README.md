# Room Content Prototype

Roll **d12 Challenge** and **d8 Opportunity** together after placing the room template. Interpret both through the site's Domain, Theme, the template's terrain tags, and its Danger Rank.

This is an initial playtest table rather than a final distribution.

## Challenge die (d12)

| d12 | Challenge | Interpretation |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Quiet aftermath | No immediate challenge. Show evidence of something that happened, changed, or recently departed. No conquest reward unless play reveals and resolves a real challenge. |
| 2 | Domain obstacle | A blocked route, sealed mechanism, damaged structure, difficult traversal, or other problem arising from the site's original function. |
| 3 | Theme hazard | The Theme manifests as an active environmental danger or worsening condition. |
| 4 | Trap | A concealed or legible mechanism, ambush device, magical ward, or environmental trigger threatens passage or interaction. |
| 5 | Puzzle | A pattern, ritual, machine, inscription, spatial problem, or rule-bound phenomenon blocks progress or conceals value. |
| 6 | Social obstacle | An intelligent occupant, prisoner, rival, survivor, or faction representative wants something incompatible with immediate passage. |
| 7 | Uncertain creature | A creature or group is present, but its initial reaction is not automatically hostile. Determine motive and reaction. |
| 8 | Hostile creature | One or more creatures immediately threaten, hunt, defend, or exploit the adventurers. |
| 9 | Creature plus Domain complication | A creature encounter is shaped by the room's original function, machinery, defenses, or inhabitants. |
| 10 | Creature plus Theme manifestation | A creature encounter is altered or supported by the site's current Theme. |
| 11 | Compound challenge | Roll twice on this table, rerolling further 11s and 12s, and combine both challenges into one situation. |
| 12 | Major threat | Generate an elite creature, severe hazard, faction leader, complex trap, or intense Theme manifestation appropriate to the current Danger Rank. |

The Challenge determines the room's primary conquest condition. Quiet aftermath does not create a condition by itself.

## Opportunity die (d8)

| d8 | Opportunity | Interpretation |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | None | The room offers no additional reward beyond passage, information inherent in the challenge, and any conquest Skill Token. |
| 2 | Clue | Information about the site's history, objective, inhabitants, route, weakness, or approaching danger. |
| 3 | Supply | A useful consumable such as light, food, bandages, ammunition, oil, antidote ingredients, or a repair component. |
| 4 | Usable feature | Machinery, cover, a movable object, a defensible position, or another room element the adventurers can exploit now or later. |
| 5 | Treasure | Wealth, trade goods, art, salvage, or a valuable object appropriate to Domain and Danger Rank. |
| 6 | Secret | A hidden compartment, concealed message, optional mechanism, secret exit, or fact not revealed by ordinary entry. |
| 7 | Shortcut | A route that bypasses rooms, creates a loop, improves retreat, or reaches another level of the site. |
| 8 | Major discovery | A relic, spellbook, exceptional item, faction-changing secret, objective component, or other campaign-relevant find. |

The Opportunity should be integrated into the Challenge where possible. A monster may carry the clue, a trap may guard the shortcut, a puzzle may operate the useful machinery, or a social obstacle may know where the treasure lies.

## Synthesis procedure

1. Place and orient the room template.
2. Note its terrain tags and Danger Rank.
3. Roll d12 Challenge and d8 Opportunity together.
4. Translate the Challenge through the Domain and Theme.
5. Place the Opportunity inside, behind, or in tension with the Challenge.
6. State enough sensory evidence for informed free-form decisions.
7. Identify the main challenge and what would count as durable resolution.
8. Use Danger Rank as the default Defense or target for generated threats.

## Examples

### Haunted Mine, pillared gallery, Standard 12

- Challenge 9: Creature plus Domain complication.
- Opportunity 2: Clue.
- Result: spectral miners repeat a lethal hauling routine between pillars and an ore lift. Their chalk tallies reveal that the missing foreman sealed something below. Negotiating a pause, disrupting the lift cycle, laying the ghosts to rest, or safely bypassing the routine could conquer the room.

### Flooded Temple, sunken pit, Hard 15

- Challenge 4: Trap.
- Opportunity 7: Shortcut.
- Result: stepping on submerged prayer tiles seals the doors and fills the pit. Holding the correct liturgical sequence opens an old drainage stair leading around several rooms.

### Infested Archive, balcony stacks, Easy 9

- Challenge 1: Quiet aftermath.
- Opportunity 6: Secret.
- Result: shredded catalog cards and molted skins cover the floor. A false index drawer hides a message and key. Unless further play reveals a live threat, searching the room provides no conquest reward.

### Clockwork Prison, rotating cells, Extreme 18

- Challenge 11: Compound—Puzzle plus Social obstacle.
- Opportunity 8: Major discovery.
- Result: rotating cells must be aligned while a centuries-old prisoner bargains, lies, and interferes from within the mechanism. The central cell contains an exceptional item or vital objective component.

## Open tuning questions

- Is one-third of results involving creatures too frequent or too rare?
- **Deferred:** whether a future Dungeon Alert system modifies the Challenge roll or triggers separate events.
- Should Danger Rank affect Opportunity quality as well as challenge difficulty?
- Should a Shortcut automatically add a map connection or require a separate topology roll?
- How are exact Domain and Theme prompts generated without slowing every room?
